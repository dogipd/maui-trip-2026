🌴 Maui Vacation Master Plan

October 25 – October 31, 2026 | Basecamp: Menehune Shores #421 (North Kihei)

Day 1: Sun, Oct 25 — Arrival & Settling In Arrival

🚗 Drive: ~15 mins from OGG 🌅 Sunset: ~5:52 PM
  • Afternoon: Land at Kahului Airport (OGG), retrieve rental car.
  • Grocery Stop: Stop at Safeway/Target in Kahului for bottled water, snacks, and breakfast essentials.
  • Check-in: Drive 15 mins south to Menehune Shores #421 in North Kihei. Settle in.
  • Evening: Walk out to the Kōʻieʻie Royal Fishpond for sunset; grab relaxed dinner downstairs at Monsoon India.
Things to Remember Today: Keep bedroom door open with living-room split A/C + ceiling fan running for sleep comfort. Lay out warm layers before going to bed early.

Day 2: Mon, Oct 26 — Haleakalā Sunrise & Pineapple Tour ✨ Xplorie Pass Day

🚗 Drive: ~1 hr 45 min to summit 🌄 Sunrise: ~6:24 AM (Leave by 3:30 AM)
🎟️ Xplorie Included Activity: Maui Pineapple Farm Tour ($75+ Value)
Guided walking & vehicle tour of working pineapple fields in Haliʻimaile. Includes tastings of extra-sweet Maui Gold pineapples and a complimentary airport-packaged pineapple to take home!
  • 3:30 AM: Depart condo in darkness (leverage mainland jet lag).
  • 5:15 AM – 7:00 AM: Park at summit, watch sunrise above cloud deck (35°F–40°F cold; bring jackets/beanies).
  • 8:00 AM – 9:30 AM: Drive down to historic Makawao town for warm pastries, fresh coffee, and breakfast.
  • 10:30 AM – 12:00 PM: Maui Pineapple Farm Tour in Haliʻimaile (Xplorie free pass). Receive airport-packaged sweet pineapple.
  • Afternoon: Return to condo for an afternoon recharge nap and pool relaxation.
  • Evening: Watch panoramic sunset and grill dinner on the 360° rooftop BBQ deck.
Things to Remember Today: Have Recreation.gov sunrise QR code and photo ID ready for national park gate entry. Pack full winter jackets and closed-toe shoes.

Day 3: Tue, Oct 27 — Road to Hāna & Pīpīwai Trail Expedition

🚗 Drive: Full Day (Paved Out-and-Back) ⏰ Departure: 5:30 AM
  • 5:30 AM: Depart condo early to beat tour vans and enjoy empty scenic roads.
  • 7:15 AM: Quick photo stop at Honomanū Bay Overlook (Mile Marker 13–14).
  • 7:45 AM – 8:30 AM: Keʻanae Peninsula: Watch crashing surf and grab warm loaves from Aunt Sandy's Banana Bread.
  • 9:00 AM – 11:30 AM: Waiʻānapanapa State Park: Black sand beach, sea arches, and blowholes.
  • 11:45 AM – 12:30 PM: Hāna Town: Lunch break at local food trucks.
  • 1:00 PM – 3:15 PM: Kīpahulu District: Hike Pīpīwai Trail (Giant Banyan, bamboo forest boardwalk, 400-ft Waimoku Falls).
  • 3:30 PM: Begin return drive along paved Hāna Highway back toward Pāʻia in full daylight.
  • 5:30 PM – 6:00 PM: Arrive back at condo before dark for dinner and rest.
Things to Remember Today: Download offline Google Maps before leaving. National park pass from Haleakalā is valid at Kīpahulu. Turn around at trail—do NOT drive the unpaved back road.

Day 4: Wed, Oct 28 — West Maui & Kaʻanapali Coast West Maui

🚗 Drive: ~40 mins north along Route 30 🌊 Focus: Coastal walks & viewpoints
  • 9:00 AM: Scenic drive north along coastal cliffs.
  • 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM: Walk the paved, scenic Kapalua Coastal Trail (ironwood groves, lava tidepools, ocean views).
  • 12:30 PM – 2:30 PM: Kaʻanapali Beachwalk and Whalers Village for lunch and shopping.
  • 3:00 PM – 4:30 PM: Scenic drive to coastal overlooks at Honolua Bay & Nakalele Blowhole.
  • Evening: Return down coast for dinner in Kihei.
Things to Remember Today: Wear comfortable walking sandals/shoes and apply reef-safe sunscreen.

Day 5: Thu, Oct 29 — Molokini Cruise & Turtle Lagoon ✨ Xplorie Pass Day

🚗 Drive: 8 mins to Māʻalaea Harbor 🐢 Focus: Marine life & Molokini reef
🎟️ Xplorie Included Activity: Calypso Catamaran Molokini Cruise ($160+ Value)
Morning excursion to Molokini Crater & Turtle Town. Features hot breakfast, lunch, complimentary snorkel gear, dual water slides, and an underwater glass-bottom viewing room!
  • 7:00 AM: Short 8-minute drive to Māʻalaea Harbor.
  • 7:30 AM – 11:30 AM: Calypso Catamaran Molokini Cruise (Xplorie free pass; snorkeling + glass-bottom viewing room).
  • 12:30 PM: Return to condo for fresh lunch and quick rinse.
  • 2:00 PM – 4:30 PM: Wade in the calm, protected waters of Kōʻieʻie Royal Fishpond to watch green sea turtles.
  • 5:30 PM: Golden hour stroll along Sugar Beach or Keawakapu Beach.
Things to Remember Today: Take ginger chews/Dramamine 30 minutes before boarding boat if sensitive to motion. Bring beach towels and reef-safe sunscreen.

Day 6: Fri, Oct 30 — ʻĪao Valley & Ulua Beach Snorkeling Rainforest & Snorkel

🚗 Drive: 20 mins to ʻĪao / 15 mins to Wailea 🤿 Focus: Walk-in Coral Reef & Rainforest
  • 9:00 AM – 11:30 AM: Paved, scenic walk at ʻĪao Valley State Monument beneath the 1,200-ft emerald needle.
  • 12:00 PM – 1:30 PM: Casual lunch in historic Wailuku or South Kihei food trucks.
  • 2:00 PM – 4:30 PM: Walk-In Snorkeling at Ulua Beach (Wailea): Easy, sandy entry leading to a vibrant coral reef ridge separating Ulua and Mokapu bays. Home to green sea turtles, butterflyfish, humuhumunukunukuāpuaʻa, and clear calm waters.
  • 5:00 PM – 6:00 PM: Rinse off and relax along the paved Wailea Beach Path.
  • Evening: Farewell sunset drinks and BBQ dinner on the 360° rooftop deck.
Things to Remember Today: Have ʻĪao Valley entry QR voucher downloaded. Ulua Beach has free paved parking, outdoor showers, and restrooms, but lots can fill by early afternoon—arrive by 1:45–2:00 PM.

Day 7: Sat, Oct 31 — Pāʻia Town & Departure Departure

🚗 Drive: 15 mins to OGG ✈️ Check-out: 10:00 AM
  • 10:00 AM: Pack up, empty fridge, and check out of Menehune Shores #421.
  • 11:00 AM – 1:30 PM: Browse shops, grab lunch in bohemian Pāʻia Town, and watch sea turtles at Hoʻokipa Beach lookout.
  • Afternoon: Return rental car at OGG and board nonstop flight back to Seattle.
