📅 Detailed Day-by-Day Master Plan

Day 1: Sunday, Oct 25 — Arrival & Basecamp Setup Māʻalaea Banyans
  • 1:19 PM: Arrive at Kahului Airport (OGG). Pick up rental vehicle.
  • 2:30 PM: Costco & Safeway Kahului grocery run for breakfast supplies and snacks.
  • 4:00 PM: Check into Māʻalaea Banyans. Unpack for a comfortable 4-night stay.
  • 5:45 PM: Sunset stroll along Sugar Beach; dinner at Monsoon India or local coastal dining.
Day 2: Monday, Oct 26 — Haleakalā Sunrise & Upcountry Pineapple Tour Xplorie Pass: Pineapple Tour
  • 3:30 AM: Depart Māʻalaea for Haleakalā Summit (dress warmly in layers).
  • 5:30 AM – 7:00 AM: Watch the sunrise above the clouds at 10,023 feet.
  • 8:30 AM: Grab hot malasadas and cream puffs at T. Komoda Store & Bakery in historic Makawao.
  • 11:45 AM – 1:15 PM: Maui Pineapple Farm Tour in Hāliʻimaile (includes airport-ready boxed pineapple!).
  • 1:45 PM: Return to Māʻalaea condo for an afternoon nap and pool lounging.
  • 5:30 PM: Relaxed oceanfront sunset BBQ on the condo lawn grills.
Day 3: Tuesday, Oct 27 — West Maui Alpacas, Whalers Village & Sunset Cliff Dive Xplorie Pass: Alpaca Tour
  • 8:30 AM: Sleep in and enjoy breakfast on the condo lanai.
  • 10:00 AM – 11:00 AM: Maui Dragon Fruit Farm Alpaca Tour in Lahaina (feed alpacas & sample tropical fruit).
  • 11:30 AM – 3:30 PM: Whalers Village & Kaʻanapali Boardwalk: Lunch at Monkeypod Kitchen / Hula Grill, explore beachfront shops, and stroll the paved coastal promenade.
  • 5:00 PM – 5:45 PM: Witness the torch-lighting and Cliff Diving Ceremony at Black Rock (Puʻu Kekaʻa).
  • 6:30 PM: Oceanfront dinner at Mala Ocean Tavern or Star Noodle before a 25-min drive back to Māʻalaea.
Day 4: Wednesday, Oct 28 — Atlantis Submarine & Pre-Hāna Afternoon Recharge Undersea Tour + Rest
  • 10:00 AM: Easy 25-minute drive to Lahaina Harbor.
  • 10:30 AM – 12:45 PM: Atlantis Submarine Dive (11:00 AM): Descend 100+ feet beneath the Pacific to explore the Carthaginian shipwreck, sea turtles, and reefs (100% non-swimmer friendly).
  • 1:00 PM – 1:45 PM: Casual lunch in Lahaina.
  • 2:00 PM: Return to Māʻalaea Banyans — done with all driving for the day!
  • 2:00 PM – 5:30 PM: Complete Rest & Downtime: Lounge by the pool/hot tub, stroll Sugar Beach, and relax.
  • 6:00 PM: Casual dinner on the lawn grills or walk to Māʻalaea Harbor restaurants. Pack overnight duffel bag for Hāna.
Day 5: Thursday, Oct 29 — Road to Hāna & Waiʻānapanapa Black Sand Beach Hāna-Maui Resort (25k pts)
  • 8:30 AM: Check out of Māʻalaea Banyans; drive toward Pāʻia to begin the scenic Road to Hāna.
  • 9:30 AM – 1:30 PM: Scenic stops: Twin Falls, Keʻanae Peninsula (Aunt Sandy’s warm banana bread), and waterfall lookouts.
  • 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM: Waiʻānapanapa State Park: Black Sand Beach, lava tube sea cave, blowhole, and coastal trails.
  • 4:30 PM: Check into Hāna-Maui Resort (King Partial Ocean View).
  • 6:00 PM: Rainforest open-air dinner at The Restaurant at Hāna-Maui followed by tranquil stargazing.
Day 6: Friday, Oct 30 — Birthday Bamboo Forest Hike & Andaz Luxury Check-In 🎂 Wife's Birthday Celebration
  • 7:30 AM – 10:00 AM: Hike Pīpīwai Trail (Giant Banyan, Bamboo Forest, 400-ft Waimoku Falls) before day-tripper crowds arrive.
  • 10:30 AM: Return to resort, hot shower, and 11:00 AM checkout.
  • 11:30 AM – 2:30 PM: Scenic daylight drive back along Hāna Highway; stop in Pāʻia town for lunch and coffee.
  • 3:30 PM: Check into Andaz Maui at Wailea Resort (1 King Bed — 80k pts). Welcome champagne toast.
  • 5:30 PM: Sunset viewing over the 4 tiered oceanfront infinity pools.
  • 7:00 PM: Intimate Birthday Dinner: Reserved oceanfront table at Kaʻana Kitchen or Morimoto Maui.
Day 7: Saturday, Oct 31 — Resort Relaxation & Oceanfront Lūʻau Wailea Beach Promenade
  • 9:00 AM – 3:30 PM: Full day of relaxation at Andaz tiered infinity pools and golden Mōkapu Beach.
  • 4:45 PM: Romantic 5-minute stroll along the paved oceanfront path to Wailea Beach Resort.
  • 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM: Te Au Moana Lūʻau: Ocean bluff seating, buffet feast, premium open bar, and 3-performer Samoan fire-knife finale.
  • 8:15 PM: Moonlit walk back to Andaz for evening drinks.
Day 8: Sunday, Nov 1 — Final Island Morning & Flight Home Departure Day
  • 8:30 AM: Breakfast at Kaʻana Kitchen; final walk along Mōkapu Beach.
  • 11:00 AM: Check out of Andaz Maui.
  • 11:45 AM: Visit Maui Tropical Plantation & Kumu Farms (Waikapū) for fresh coffee, tropical fruits, and souvenirs.
  • 1:45 PM: Refuel at Costco Kahului; return rental vehicle.
  • 2:15 PM: Arrive at OGG Airport with TSA PreCheck.
  • 4:12 PM: Nonstop flight departure to Seattle (SEA).
