  • 8:30 AM: Check out of Māʻalaea Banyans with main luggage locked securely out of sight in trunk.

  • 11:30 AM – 12:15 PM: 🍌 Aunt Sandy’s Famous Banana Bread (Keʻanae Peninsula - MM 16.8): Turn down to Keʻanae to watch huge surf crash against black lava rock while enjoying warm, fresh-out-of-the-oven banana bread.

  • 4:15 PM: Check into Hāna-Maui Resort — 1 King Ocean View Bungalow (648 sq ft). Savor the tranquil breeze and ocean views from your private lanai.
  • 6:00 PM: Rainforest open-air dinner at The Restaurant at Hāna-Maui followed by dark-sky stargazing.
